# E2E test set: scam research note

Written 2026-10-10 by an independent test author, before and without reading any KudiGuard code, rules, prompts or
existing test sets. Sources come from web searches run on 2026-10-10. Dates are publication dates where the source
showed one. "Undated" means the search result did not show a date. Several items are older than 2025 but are still
cited in 2025–2026 alerts as live patterns. The `scam_type` ids used in `cases.yaml` are in `code` style.

v2 (2026-10-10) applies pre-scoring corrections from an independent fact audit; no system output was seen. It also
adds the CBN new-device rule and the new v2 scam types.

## Rules that change what "correct advice" is

* **CBN new-device and new-app limit.** A circular dated 12 Mar 2026 (Leadership and MSME Africa print 2025) took
  effect on 1 Jul 2026. It caps transactions from a newly activated mobile banking app or a new device at
  **N20,000 for the first 24 hours**. For new accounts the cap covers inflows and outflows. For existing customers on a
  new device it covers outflows only. The circular also adds device binding and stronger first-login authentication on
  a new device. It applies to banks and to Opay, PalmPay, Moniepoint and Paga. Separately, the instant-payment rules
  allow credits to stay pending for up to 24 h. So "there is no 24-hour delay" is not safe advice; the safe rule is
  "release goods only when the money shows in your own account". Sources: BusinessDay
  https://businessday.ng/news/article/cbn-caps-new-mobile-banking-transfers-at-n20000-for-24-hours/ ; Leadership
  https://leadership.ng/cbn-caps-mobile-banking-transactions-at-n20000-within-first-24-hours-on-new-devices/ ;
  Afriwise https://www.afriwise.com/blog/device-binding-and-transaction-caps-the-cbns-instant-payment-guidelines-take-effect ;
  Proshare https://proshare.co/articles/cbn-introduces-new-security-measures-and-customer-controls-for-instant-payments .
  The auditor also cited Vanguard (Mar 2026); I did not retrieve that article.
* **CBN on what banks never ask.** The CBN's 2015 BVN statement says the CBN, banks and their staff will never call or
  text customers asking for **passwords, card details or PIN**. Banks' own advisories (e.g. Access) extend this to
  OTP, CVV and BVN. CBN 2015: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2015/CCD/CBN%20Press%20Statement%20on%20BVN.pdf ; Access Bank
  via TheCable (undated) https://www.thecable.ng/?p=463366
* **US DV lottery.** People born in Nigeria (to Nigerian parents) were not eligible for DV-2026. Nigeria was the only
  African country excluded. I could not confirm the DV-2027 list, so the advice is to check travel.state.gov. Sources:
  Legit https://www.legit.ng/people/1729296-us-green-card-lottery-1-african-country-barred-participating-2026/ ; CrispNG
  https://crispng.com/why-nigeria-only-african-country-excluded-dv-2026-dv-2027-outlook/
* **Complaints about a SIM or line go to the network first, then NCC 622.** NCC says 622 is the second-level
  escalation after the operator. Sources: https://ncc.gov.ng/node/1330 ; https://ncc.gov.ng/node/1302
