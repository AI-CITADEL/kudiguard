🛡️ KudiGuard Privacy Notice

Effective 10 October 2026 · Operated by {{OPERATOR}} · Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023

What KudiGuard does

KudiGuard checks messages and voice notes for signs of money scams and answers money-safety questions, on WhatsApp, on this website and through an API for partner organisations. It gives guidance only, not financial advice.

What we collect and keep

  • Your phone number is kept only in a one-way coded form (a keyed hash), so we can recognise returning users without storing the number. On the website we use a random session id instead.
  • Your messages and our replies, with most phone, account and card numbers and email addresses automatically masked. Other details you type, such as names or addresses, are kept as written, so please leave them out.
  • Our scam assessment, an English translation of your message, the channel used (WhatsApp or website), your language, when you agreed to this notice, your 👍/👎 feedback, and timing information.
  • Voice notes are turned into text and then deleted; the audio itself is not stored, unless you choose to donate your voice notes (see Voice-note donation).

We do not ask for your contacts, location or photos, and we never ask for PINs, OTPs or passwords.

Why (legal basis)

To answer you, to improve the service and measure how well it works, and to protect others from scams (including improving KudiGuard's scam detection and, after review, training its models), based on your consent, which you give by continuing after our welcome message (or ticking the box on this website). Voice-note donation has its own, separate consent (below); KudiGuard works the same without it.

Voice-note donation (optional)

You can choose to let us keep your voice notes to test and improve how well KudiGuard understands Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo and Nigerian English speech. It is off unless you say yes: on WhatsApp we ask once, after one of your voice notes has been answered, and you can say yes with the "Yes, I am 18+" button (or send DONATE VOICE to see the offer); on this website, tick the separate donation box.

  • Who can donate: adults (18 or older) only, and only voice notes in your own voice. Please do not donate recordings of other people. Voice notes you forward on WhatsApp are never kept, and text messages are never donated.
  • What we keep: each voice note you send after saying yes, as you sent it, encrypted, with a random id, the coded form of your number or website session (so you can withdraw), your language, the channel, the week it arrived (not the time), its length when known, the version of the offer you accepted, and our automatic transcript (most numbers masked). On WhatsApp we also record when you said yes. Voice notes you sent before saying yes are never kept.
  • What it is used for: only to test and improve speech recognition. A few trusted reviewers and transcribers (our team, or native speakers engaged under written confidentiality) listen to a sample and write down what was said, so we can measure and reduce errors. We never use your voice to identify you or to recognise who is speaking, and we never sell, publish or share the recordings with anyone else.
  • Where: recordings are stored only on KudiGuard's own server, never in third-party cloud storage. Transcribers work on copies on their own devices without uploading them anywhere, and delete them once the transcripts are typed.
  • How long: up to {{VOICE_RETENTION}}, then deleted automatically.
  • Stop at any time: on WhatsApp send STOP DONATING (or tap "No, thanks"); on this website untick the box in the same browser. This stops collection and deletes every voice note you donated (from that browser, on the website), and you get a confirmation. DELETE also deletes them. Sending STOP ends donation too (voice notes already donated then stay until they expire, you send STOP DONATING or DELETE, or you ask us).

Who processes it

  • The AI models (N-ATLAS and the NCAIR1 speech models) run on KudiGuard's own server. Your messages are not sent to outside AI companies.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) carries messages between you and us; Cloudflare provides the secure network connection to our server. Each follows its own privacy policy and may process data outside Nigeria under its own safeguards.
  • Reviewers: to check the quality and safety of our answers, a few trusted reviewers (our team, or native speakers engaged under written confidentiality) may read a sample of de-identified messages and replies (most numbers masked, and without your phone number or any id of you). Their copies are deleted after the check. Donated voice notes are also heard by such reviewers and transcribers (see above).
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising. Anonymised totals (for example, how many checks were made in each language) may be published.

How long

  • Messages and replies: deleted automatically {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days after they were sent.
  • Your settings (language, consent): deleted {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days after your last message, or, if you donated voice notes, once none of them is left (they record your donation consent).
  • Donated voice notes: up to {{VOICE_RETENTION}} (see above).
  • Scam examples: when a message is judged a likely scam or suspicious, a separate copy is kept for up to {{PATTERN_RETENTION}} to protect others and improve KudiGuard: its wording and English translation (most numbers, emails and card numbers masked; most links reduced to the website name), the language, channel, our assessment and the week, without your number or any id of you or the conversation. While your own messages are still stored ({{RETENTION_DAYS}} days), DELETE also removes the examples taken from them; after that they can no longer be matched to you.

Your rights

  • On WhatsApp, send DELETE to erase everything linked to you, immediately, including donated voice notes.
  • Send STOP DONATING (or untick the box on this website) to withdraw your voice-note donation and delete what you donated.
  • Send STOP to stop receiving messages; your data is then kept only until it expires or you send DELETE.
  • To access, correct or delete your data (including from the website, and including a copy of your donated voice notes), or to object to its use: {{CONTACT}}.
  • You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Data is protected in transit (HTTPS), phone numbers are stored only in coded form, donated voice notes are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM), and data is kept on access-controlled systems. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will inform the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours, and you directly when the risk is high. Changes to this notice will be announced to users before they take effect.
