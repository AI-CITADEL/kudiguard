Effective 8 October 2026 · Operated by {{OPERATOR}} · Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023

What KudiGuard does

KudiGuard checks messages and voice notes for signs of money scams and answers money-safety questions, on WhatsApp, on this website and through an API for partner organisations. It gives guidance only, not financial advice.

What we collect and keep

  • Your phone number is kept only in a one-way coded form (a keyed hash), so we can recognise returning users without storing the number. On the website we use a random session id instead.
  • Your messages and our replies, with most phone, account and card numbers and email addresses automatically masked. Other details you type, such as names or addresses, are kept as written, so please leave them out.
  • Our scam assessment, an English translation of your message, the channel used (WhatsApp or website), your language, when you agreed to this notice, your 👍/👎 feedback, and timing information.
  • Voice notes are turned into text and then deleted; the audio itself is not stored.

We do not ask for your contacts, location or photos, and we never ask for PINs, OTPs or passwords.

Why (legal basis)

To answer you, to improve the service and measure how well it works, and to protect others from scams (including improving KudiGuard's scam detection and, after review, training its models), based on your consent, which you give by continuing after our welcome message (or ticking the box on this website).

Who processes it

  • The AI models (N-ATLAS and the NCAIR1 speech models) run on KudiGuard's own server. Your messages are not sent to outside AI companies.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) carries messages between you and us; Cloudflare provides the secure network connection to our server. Each follows its own privacy policy and may process data outside Nigeria under its own safeguards.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising. Anonymised totals (for example, how many checks were made in each language) may be published.

How long

  • Messages and replies: deleted automatically {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days after they were sent.
  • Your settings (language, consent): deleted {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days after your last message.
  • Scam examples: when a message is judged a likely scam or suspicious, a separate copy is kept for up to {{PATTERN_RETENTION}} to protect others and improve KudiGuard: its wording and English translation (most numbers, emails and card numbers masked; most links reduced to the website name), the language, channel, our assessment and the week, without your number or any id of you or the conversation. While your own messages are still stored ({{RETENTION_DAYS}} days), DELETE also removes the examples taken from them; after that they can no longer be matched to you.

Your rights

  • On WhatsApp, send DELETE to erase everything linked to you, immediately.
  • Send STOP to stop receiving messages; your data is then kept only until it expires or you send DELETE.
  • To access, correct or delete your data (including from the website), or to object to its use: {{CONTACT}}.
  • You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Data is protected in transit (HTTPS), phone numbers are stored only in coded form, and data is kept on access-controlled systems. Changes to this notice will be announced to users before they take effect.
